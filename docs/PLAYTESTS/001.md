# Playtest 001 — canon-v1.0 balance pass (simulation campaign)

**Date:** 2026-07-09 (session 006)
**Rules version:** v2.2 (canon-v1.0) — `prisonDecayPerUnit` 1 → 2 shipped this pass (decision 55)
**Decks:** Crimson Assault (red, post-churn) vs Radiant Order (yellow, post-churn)
**Method:** headless sims — heuristic and random policies, alternating seats, seeds 1000+, N=100–300
per configuration. The heuristic bot was upgraded mid-pass (see finding 2); numbers before/after the
upgrade are not comparable.

## Findings

1. **The card churn alone broke red** (18% in heuristic mirrors, down from ~36% pre-churn). The
   yellow re-costs — especially the cheaper utility actions — outweighed red's gains, and red's
   deck-doubles rule had silently swapped its two doubled workhorse bodies for utility actions
   (fixed: doubles are the cheap core minus Warpath/Pillage, plus Rageforged Brute + Volcanic Slam).

2. **The bot was lying to us.** The greedy heuristic scored targeted plays without looking at the
   target — an imprison on a 1/1 scored the same as on the 8/8, burn was effectively
   random-targeted, and killing a jailer (which frees every captive) carried no value. After making
   it target-aware, red jumped 18% → 38% *with zero card changes*. **Moral: sim conclusions are
   bounded by policy quality; re-verify balance claims after any bot change.**

3. **Prison is the structural dominator** (ablation: removing all imprison effects → red 44%,
   influence wins 14%). Not the bombs, not the recurring jailers — the core recurring lockup loop.
   Doubling the decay (the "mortgage") was the clean, theme-positive fix: mass imprisonment is now
   a real bet against your own win axis. Red 38% → ~46% with decay 2 alone.

4. **Guard's free interception — the handoff's prime suspect — is nearly irrelevant** to the
   matchup (ablating the keyword moved red ~1 point). The "too sticky" worry can rest.

5. **Sample noise is real at N=300:** the shipped package measured 45.7% red on one deck-ordering
   and 55.3% on another (a cost change reorders the generated deck list, which changes every
   shuffle). Honest claim: **red ~45–55%, even within bot noise.**

## UX audit (same session — fresh-eyes browser drive of the demo)

**Fixed (verified live in Chrome):**
- **Second-click-plays trap (Blaine's report):** the hand-card "tap-again-to-confirm" shortcut
  meant a habitual double-click was select+confirm — "let me read this card" became "banked
  forever" (reproduced on camera: an inspect attempt banked Rageforged Brute). Now the second tap
  *deselects*; playing/banking is only ever the explicit button.
- **Inspect vs. act ambiguity (Blaine's report):** inspecting a hand card or an actionable unit
  required a long-press — undiscoverable with a mouse, and the sidebar tip claimed "tap any card
  to inspect," which was false exactly for the cards you most want to read. Now: **right-click =
  inspect everywhere** (cards, units — desktop mirror of long-press), the hand-selection panel
  gained an "ⓘ details" button, and the tip tells the truth ("tap selects/targets; right-click or
  long-press inspects").

**Observed good:** the landing explains the three modes + quick how-to; unplayable cards dim and
say *why* when selected ("costs 3, you have 2 ready resources"); the round/turn status line,
auto-pass, undo, and the influence track read clearly; the card inspector surfaces each card's
design-note rulings in-game.

**Noted, not fixed (low severity):** the intercept prompt is wordy on mobile widths; long-press
discovery still depends on reading the tip. (Correction on re-audit: Esc already cancels
selection/targeting/inspect — the earlier "would be nice" note was wrong.)

## Addendum 3 — the code review that re-ran the night (loop iterations 2–3)

A high-effort review workflow over the session diff surfaced 10 confirmed defects — three of them
in the *new* bot: no scoring case for `damageFilter` (board wipes never cast), no penalty for
destroying your own unit (Execution Swing suicides), and a sign-blind curse penalty (purple's
Wither/Pacify never cast). **Every balance number above was measured with those bugs in.**

Re-measured after the fixes (N=300 each): red–yellow **58%** red at decay 2, **44.3%** at decay 1;
purple–yellow **70%** at decay 2, **57.7%** at decay 1; purple–red 49.7% (prison-free matchup).
Decay 1 also brings the influence economy alive (4.3–10% upsets vs ~2%). Conclusion: decision 55's
doubling was compensating for a bot that couldn't fight prisons — reverted as **rules v2.3,
decision 56**. Finding 2's moral got proven twice in one night.

Also fixed from the review: stale-DB seeding (reseeds now refresh cards/rules/decks — merged card
PRs actually reach the server), duplicate-slug and deck-legality gates in `npm run cards:check`,
locale-independent generated.json ordering, the influenceTrigger dial on start-of-round engines,
purple plumbing in the web app's browser/admin, and stale version stamps in the demo.
