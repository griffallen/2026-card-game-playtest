# The Color Wheel — Identity Charter

**Source:** the designer, [issue #10](https://github.com/booherbg/2026-card-game/issues/10)
(2026-07-10). Decision 65. This is the reference the v3 card re-churn — and every future
card — designs against. Wording below preserves the designer's framing.

**Deck philosophy:** mono-color must be playable; multi-color creativity is actively wanted.
(The pip system's splash tax — `06-PIP-PROPOSAL.md` — is the economy that makes both true.)

## The five colors

| Color | Identity | Game plan | How it wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RED** — Rage | Aggressive burst; aggression + snowball | The pure pressure engine: damage early, punish slow starts, snowball unchecked. It doesn't just attack — it *escalates*. | Fast Life damage; momentum chains ("Surge-style play") |
| **YELLOW** — Honor & Protection | Efficiency and defense; aggression through optimization | Smart aggression: still pushes forward, but via efficiency, conversion, and tempo — not raw force. | Better unit trades; efficiency |
| **PURPLE** — Wisdom, Magic, Shadow | Control through insight and timing | Reactive control: reads the opponent, disrupts key plays, stabilizes until it takes over. | Counterplay; predictive effects; turning opponent actions into advantage |
| **BLUE** — Machines, Intelligence, Engineering | Control via systems | Controls through structure and repetition; doesn't stop everything — makes everything *inefficient* for the opponent. | Engine building; **incremental Influence gain**; out-valuing over time |
| **GREEN** — Nature, Growth | Midrange; a little of everything | The anchor: builds a board, controls space, wins by becoming overwhelming. Balanced, board-centric inevitability. | Strong units and zone presence; stabilizes vs aggro, pressures control |

## Costing principle (decision 68, issue #4)

**Rates scale superlinearly with cost** — never 1-for-1 linear, never exponential. High-cost
cards (5–7) must "do a lot": the premium compensates for card economy (one card played is one
card spent) and is bought twice under pips (more resources *and* more color commitment). The
session-006 linear burn grammar is superseded. Exact curves are re-churn + playtest work.

## Implications already on the record

- **Influence mapping (decision 63, #6):** red and yellow are the least influence-interactive;
  blue's charter names incremental Influence explicitly — blue is *the* influence-engine color.
  Purple's route, if any, should be **reactive** influence (gain by counterplay/aggression — e.g.
  Sneak payloads that drain or steal) so it never duplicates blue's accumulation or yellow's
  civic gain. Open on the thread.
- **Engine constraint → purple's shape:** the engine never pauses mid-resolution (decision 24),
  so purple's "reactive control" wants **predict/trap shapes** (committed in advance, sprung on
  the trigger — Hidden and Sneak already live here) rather than interrupt shapes (respond
  mid-play). Raised on #10 as a design consequence, not a veto.
- **"Surge-style" momentum chains (red):** possible future keyword; not in the v3 set. Flagged
  on #10 for definition whenever red's charter gets its churn pass.
- **Sequencing (proposed on #10):** v3 re-churn rebuilds red/yellow/purple to their charters →
  canon-v2.0; **blue and green are born after**, as the first expansion designed against v3
  mechanics from day one.
