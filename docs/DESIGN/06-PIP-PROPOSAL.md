# Pip Assignment Proposal — v3.0 resource economy (decision-58-era draft)

**Status:** proposal for playtesting, per the designer's ask on
[issue #9](https://github.com/booherbg/2026-card-game/issues/9) (Q3): mono-color pips only for
now; **more same-color pips = harder to splash = the balance lever for big/good cards** (cf. MTG
CC costs, SWU aspects). Slash at will — every number here is a default, not a verdict.

## The grammar (baseline rule)

| Cost | Pips | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 1 | cheap cards splash freely — one matching resource |
| 3–6 | 2 | the midgame demands commitment |
| 7+ | 3 | finishers are faction statements |

Plus **13 hand-tuned deviations** (below, with reasons): identity lynchpins tax harder
than their cost (Warcry Leader, Wither, Veilmaster, Gateward Colossus); cheap generic
interaction taxes lighter so future multi-color decks have splash targets.

## Knobs playtesting can turn
- The tier boundaries (does 2-pip start at 3 or 4?).
- Whether cheap interaction (burn, tricks) should stay 1-pip or tax at 2 to protect faction identity.
- Which cards join the future **0-pip** class (true colorless utility — none proposed yet).
- Purple rows are provisional twice over (the deck itself is a proposal).
