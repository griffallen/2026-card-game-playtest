## Summary

The longest and most consequential watch session yet. Griff playtested hard and the game
changed shape under him — decisions 89 through 100 all landed in this one session.

**New engine vocabulary, taught by cards.** The session began mid-fold of PRs #38/#39:
Final Onslaught's `doom` (the readied unit and everything its attack wounded die after the
extra action) and Unchained Rage's `attackTax` (influence per attacker while the rage lasts),
decision 89. Then the streak continued: Griff's Reckless Abandon request (#45) built **X costs**
end-to-end (`cost: X` frontmatter, declared at cast, `xSurge` op, X-picker UI, AI scoring —
decision 91). His Fiery Impaler PR (#46) built the game's first **chosen trigger target**
(`splashReap` + attack-carried `splash` declarations, Door A after a held review — decision 93).
Prison Warrant (#53) added `damagedOrMaxHealth` targeting and **capture income** (rent while
held — decision 96). Vanguard Sentinel (#54) added the **`onDeath` trigger** (built delete-first
after the first version recursed — decision 97).

**Rules court rulings.** Decision 90: decks are min-48, not exactly-48 (the tooling gate
relaxed; Cataclysmic Charge doubles again; red runs 49). Decision 92: **death is the only
key** — voluntary capture-release and the grip-lock both died ("easier to play"). Decision 94:
**Scar uncapped** (+1 per damage, no limit — superseding 70; his zero-attack Berserker screenshot
arrived two minutes after the fix deployed). Decision 95: defense survives the initiative claim
(ratified as-is; the misleading badge now reads "claimed — resting, still defends").

**The big one — decision 98, the duel law.** Griff, tired of chump-soaks: "if I target a unit,
it must get hit." Built behind a rules knob, A/B'd over 400 games (red 28.5% → 36.3%), and
canonized on his "flip it." A lone attacker cannot be blocked except by ONE Guard (full
redirect, no exhaust); gangs stay open. Ten test fixtures converted to canon form. Then
decision 100 carved out the Home: base attacks keep open blocking (purple owns zero Guards
and literally couldn't defend its base) — measured cost, red 46% → 33.5% on the prebuilt.

**The yellow rebalance (#55, decision 99).** Griff commissioned a full agent pass: the
defend-payout ladder came back the decision-85 notch, fourteen flat influence riders were cut,
Sentence went 4→5, Supreme Sentence hits 3. First iteration hit red 49.5% but killed the
influence win entirely (5%) — the +2 came back to the two 7-cost finishers, settling at
red 46%, influence 14%. His own trims folded alongside (Iron Plating, Oath of Order,
Prison Warrant, Vanguard Sentinel-the-martyr).

**Griff's Red ships.** He posted his 65-card curation as screenshots; it was transcribed,
simmed (43.8% vs Radiant Order — ten points better than the auto-built prebuilt), and shipped
as a named prebuilt deck so the AI can pilot it — the red benchmark for all future sims.

**Demo UX, driven by his playtests:** combat recap overlay (#42, one line at a time; second
pass holds for ✓ Continue when YOUR units leave play), the ⊘ disarmed tell (#40 — Disarming
Order was invisible), the Volcanic Slam double-fix (#41 the eager collector, then #56 the
order-sensitivity bug — picks now match as a set and partial casts never silently fire),
modal-card tooltips + legend (#43), deck-list pip sigils + plus buttons and a draggable
workshop split (#30), newest-first discards and a hand scrollbar (#48).

**Watch-loop lesson (from Blaine, mid-session):** the comments feed alone misses brand-new
issues — the sweep is now open-issues-by-updated (50) + open PRs + comments. Saved to memory.
