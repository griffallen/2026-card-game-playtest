## Summary

The night the canon got built. Session 005 (Opus) had designed the canon & card-authoring system
but the trail was briefly lost — this session recovered it from origin, reviewed the spec with
fresh eyes (approving its shape, redlining two things), and then executed the whole program.

The review's big change: the card ledger became **one markdown file per card** instead of one CSV
(`data/cards/<color>/<slug>.md`, frontmatter + card text) — because the field Griff edits most
(card text) is the field CSV is worst at, a broken edit's blast radius is one card, and every card
gets its own git history and GitHub page. Blaine ratified it, then added scope mid-flight: a
fresh-eyes UX audit of the demo, a real red/yellow balance effort, a stab at purple, and a
full-game sync. Also ratified: Griff's two channels are **card PRs + intent issues**, triaged with
`gh` at session start (now in CLAUDE.md).

What shipped, in order: the rules-canon sprint (decisions 46–54; game-rules out of `-proto`);
red + yellow **charters** — the missing middle layer, with statline grammars derived from the
actual pool; the **ledger flip** (pure parser/serializer in the engine, `npm run cards` builds
`generated.json` + `INDEX.md`, byte-identical equality proof, drift-guard test, TS card arrays /
overrides / CSV all retired); the **card churn** — all 84 cards reconciled to their two parents
(inert Overextend stripped from 19 red actions, dead perm-Rush grants rebuilt, the prison ladder
re-priced with one job per rung, the armor suite re-costed, honest auto-target text everywhere);
and then the **balance campaign**, which produced the session's two best findings: the greedy bot
was *target-blind* (fixing it moved red 18%→38% with zero card changes — sim conclusions are
bounded by policy quality), and **prison was the structural dominator** (ablation-proven), cured
thematically by doubling the decay mortgage (**rules v2.2**, decision 55). Final: red ~45–55%,
even within bot noise.

Then the extras: the **UX audit** reproduced Blaine's double-click complaint on camera (the
tap-again-to-confirm shortcut banked a card during an inspect attempt) — killed it, added
right-click-inspects-everywhere, fixed the lying tooltip. And **purple**: the Veiled Court, a
complete 36-card third-deck proposal (ranged/flying/untargetable, permanent wither, kill-only
influence) built from existing mechanics only, with a deterministic SVG art direction, tuned to a
deliberate soft triangle (47.5% vs red, 65% vs yellow). Full-game sync verified: server seeds
120 cards / 3 decks, healthz green, web production-builds.
