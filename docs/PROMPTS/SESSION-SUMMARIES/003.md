## Summary

The turn-structure rework session. Blaine designed the new system in conversation: shared rounds in the
Star Wars: Unlimited mold — per-player start steps (initiative holder first: decay, start-of-round
triggers, ready all, draw 2, optional bank), then strictly alternating single actions until both pass,
with a claimable initiative token that costs your action and ends your round but hands you the first
move next round. Summoning sickness died: units enter ready, and Rush was re-anchored to the original
note ("may move without exhausting the turn it enters play") with attack-coverage as a toggle — which
also quietly fixes the granted-Rush-on-veterans trap card from playtest finding 6.

Along the way we discovered decision 38's multi-unit-attack draft was referenced everywhere but written
nowhere — so it was re-drafted here, deliberately lean: N attackers in one zone combine into one hit
(armor applies once — massing beats armor by design), and the defender answers exactly one prompt, the
intercept window (redirect the whole attack to one ready unit; Guard now means "intercepts without
exhausting" and its forced targeting is gone). Counter-damage auto-assigns to the biggest attacker;
every contested choice is a rules parameter. Final Onslaught's "extra turn" — meaningless in shared
rounds — became "ready one of your units, then take an extra action" after Blaine caught that a
full-board ready would multiply into a second alpha strike under unlimited actions. The round-1 draw
asymmetry was dropped (alternation + claim-cost dissolve the first-mover advantage it compensated).

The spec pass rewrote game-rules.md to v2.0-proto (with seven surfaced judgment calls, all blessed),
touched simulation.md and api-endpoints.md, and produced a nine-task, fully code-specified
implementation plan for handoff to another model — Blaine is near his usage cap and passing
implementation to Opus. The demo's Audit page gained a designer-facing card describing the redesign
(with an honest "the demo still runs v1.2" note) and was deployed.

## Decisions

40. Round-based turns with claimable initiative (SWU-style) — builder; supersedes the v1.2 phase ladder.
41. Units enter ready; Rush = exhaust-free move on entry round, `rushCoversAttack` toggle (default off) — builder, restoring the original Rush note.
42. Multi-unit attack + intercept, lean v1 (one prompt, armor-once, auto counter-assignment, Guard = free intercept, forced targeting removed) — builder, honoring the designer's decision-38 ask.
43. "Extra turn" re-templates to "ready one unit + extra action" (Final Onslaught) — builder; full-board ready rejected as unbounded under unlimited actions.
44. No round-1 draw asymmetry; `firstRoundDraw` kept as a nerf lever — builder.
(Also: seven spec-level judgment calls recorded in the spec + session transcript: all-Ranged groups for
adjacent targets, Breakthrough sums, all attackers get onKill credit, interceptor stands in the target's
zone, holder may claim-to-lock, end-of-round order triggers→OE→expiry, summoningSickness kept as legacy
parameter defaulting false. Decision-38 draft ruleset found to be unrecorded — re-drafted as 42.)
