## Sheet: Narrative

- I am looking at creating a deckbuilder card game that uses some fo the elements of other games that I enjoy
- The games will take anywhere from 10-30 minutes
- The deck will consist of 50-80 cards
- A win condition will be removing all hit points from a player's 'home'
- A win condition will be to discover or complete a certain number of achievements
-  | this is similar to the decipher lord of the rings tcg
- Resources will be the various cards in the deck as cards will have multiple purposes
- I want a color based resource system
- I want to draw multiple cards per turn or at least feel like I am using my deck during a game
- I want to have interaction between players each round and allow players to respond to what is happening
- I want units or characters to move around the play area, but I don't want a specific grid that has to be adhered to
- I was thinking to use the 7 colors of the rainbow as resource colors, each with their own attribute
-  | Red | rage
-  | Orange | hubris
-  | Yellow | trade
-  | Green | nature
-  | Blue | machines
-  | Indigo | magic
-  | Purple | wisdom
- I want play to build over time as more resources have more abilities
- I want players to be able to use skill but always have a way back in
- I need to figure out a combat system that is fun but not overly complicated
- Not all characters or actions need to use combat
- Given these parameters, what are 10 card suggestions for the Red deck

## Sheet: Purple Cards

- The Purple Deck (12 Unique Cards - Counter Removed)
- Purple remains focused on wisdom, hand disruption, and decay. Instead of a hard "Counter" spell, Purple now uses a disruptive tax mechanic and punitive response damage on the Stack to stop Red's fast plays.
- Units (6)
- Card 1: Mind-Stitcher (Cost: 1 | Stats: 1 Power, 2 Health)
- Text: When this unit enters the battlefield, look at the opponent's hand.
- Card 2: Essence Drifter (Cost: 2 | Stats: 2 Power, 2 Health)
- Text: When this unit deals damage to an enemy unit, your player total recovers 1 Life.
- Card 3: Mnemonic Parasite (Cost: 3 | Stats: 2 Power, 4 Health)
- Text: When this unit is destroyed, the opponent must choose and discard a card from their hand.
- Card 4: Zone Warden (Cost: 3 | Stats: 1 Power, 5 Health)
- Text: Enemy units cannot move out of the zone this unit occupies unless they pay 1 resource.
- Card 5: Dread Knight (Cost: 4 | Stats: 4 Power, 4 Health)
- Text: When this unit kills an enemy unit, shift Influence 2 steps toward yourself.
- Card 6: Archon of Decay (Cost: 5 | Stats: 4 Power, 6 Health)
- Text: At the start of the round, deal 1 damage to all units in adjacent zones.
- Modal Events (6)
- Card 7: Gaze of the Abyss (Cost: 1)
- Action Mode: The opponent discards a card.
- Response Mode: Look at the top 3 cards of your deck, put one into your hand, and discard the rest.
- Card 8: Vile Feast (Cost: 2)
- Action Mode: Deal 2 damage to a unit. Deal 2 damage to the opponent's Life total.
- Response Mode: Target unit gets -2 Power until the end of the round.
- Card 9: Mental Tax (Cost: 2)
- Action Mode: None. (Response Only).
- Response Mode: Play on the Stack when an opponent plays an Event. They must pay 2 additional resources, or that Event is returned to their hand unresolved.
- Card 10: Essence Extractor (Cost: 3)
- Action Mode: Destroy a unit you control. Draw 3 cards.
- Response Mode: Destroy a unit you control to deal 3 damage to an attacking unit on the Stack.
- Card 11: Withering Blast (Cost: 3)
- Action Mode: Deal 4 damage to a unit. If it dies, gain 4 Life.
- Response Mode: Deal 2 damage to a unit on the Stack as it is being played. (It enters the battlefield already damaged).
- Card 12: Final Requiem (Cost: 5)
- Action Mode: Destroy ALL units in the Neutral Zone.
- Response Mode: None. (Heavy Action Only).
- Card 13: Veil-Stalker (Cost: 2 | Stats: 2 Power, 1 Health)
- Text: When this unit moves into a new zone, look at a random card in your opponent’s hand.
- Card 14: Siphon Soul (Cost: 4)
- Action Mode: Choose an enemy unit. Deal damage to it equal to the number of cards in your hand.
- Response Mode: When a friendly unit is destroyed in combat, recover Life equal to that unit's original Power.
