## Design notes

2026-07-11 (v3 churn pass 2): the colossus keeps its ghost-fortress feel — Hidden while ready, and Shielded so even a revealed colossus shrugs the first blow. A 6-cost that does a lot (decision 68).

2026-07-19 (#122, designer): dropped Hidden ("odd that a colossus would be hidden") and gave it Infiltrate — a fortress that can march into any zone. Stats to 6/6/6 (the mark). Shielded stays (Griff didn't touch it — the fortress half of the old identity). No new engine work: both keywords already exist.
